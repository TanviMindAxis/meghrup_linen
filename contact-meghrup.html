What is the minimum order quantity for wholesale pricing?

Minimum quantities depend on product type. Most hospitality categories qualify for wholesale pricing from practical bulk volumes. Share your requirement and we will suggest the best slab for your budget.

Can you customize dimensions, fabric quality and brand styling?

Yes. We offer custom sizing, GSM options, weave preferences, and branding-oriented selections so your linen program matches your property standards and guest experience goals.

How long does production and delivery usually take?

Turnaround varies by quantity and customization level. Standard orders are processed faster, while custom programs take additional time for quality checks. We provide clear timelines before confirmation.

Do you provide samples before confirming a large order?

Yes, sample support is available for serious business requirements. This helps you evaluate texture, finish, and durability before finalizing bulk procurement.

What payment terms do you offer for business clients?

Payment terms are structured according to order volume and engagement type. We discuss transparent milestone or advance-based terms at the quotation stage.

Can you handle repeat or monthly linen supply requirements?

Absolutely. We work with long-term supply schedules for hotels and hospitality groups to maintain consistency in quality, inventory flow, and delivery planning.
