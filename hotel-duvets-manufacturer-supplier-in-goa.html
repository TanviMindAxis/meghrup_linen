Add air-conditioned rooms, cooler December nights and a few guests who feel the chill more than others, and the duvet becomes one of the quietly important items on a Goa hotel bed.

Meghrup Linen & Décor is a manufacturer, supplier and wholesaler of hotel duvets, based in Pune since 2016. We supply single and double duvets and comforters, along with duvet covers and protectors, to hotels, resorts and guesthouses across Maharashtra and Goa.

What Are Hotel Duvets?

A hotel duvet is a soft, filled insert that sits inside a removable cover. Together they form the top layer of the bed. The cover is the part guests see and touch, while the duvet inside stays protected and out of the daily wash.

For a Goa property, that arrangement is practical. Covers wash and dry quickly in humid weather, duvets stay clean for longer, and housekeeping can turn a bed in minutes. Buyers still check size, filling and stitching before ordering in numbers.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Should every room have a duvet, or only on request?

Many hotels put duvets in air-conditioned rooms as standard and offer them on request elsewhere. Decide by room type, then keep a small reserve.

2. How do I freshen duvets after the monsoon closure?

Air them in a dry, ventilated space, check for damp or odours and replace anything that doesn't recover. Fresh covers on top make the bed look new.

3. Do international guests expect duvets?

Many are used to them at home, so a duvet on the bed feels familiar and reassuring. It's a small detail that guests from cooler countries often appreciate.

4. Can a duvet suit rooms where guests prefer a fan to the AC?

Yes. Guests can fold it back or use it only on cooler nights. Offering a lighter option in reserve helps those who run warm.

5. Should a large villa bed use one duvet or two singles?

Both approaches are used. Two singles can suit twin-style layouts, while a double often suits one large bed. Share your bed sizes with Meghrup and ask for advice.
