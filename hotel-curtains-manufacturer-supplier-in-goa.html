What Are Hotel Curtains?

Hotel curtains are window and door coverings made for hotels, resorts, villas and similar stays. They look like curtains in a good home, but they work harder: opened and closed all day, handled by housekeeping and exposed to coastal light and humidity.

Hotels usually choose between blackout curtains, which block most outside light, and printed curtains, which bring colour and pattern. Buyers compare fabric, length, finish and how easily curtains can be cleaned before ordering for a property.

Applications of Hotel Curtains

From open beach resorts to historic Portuguese pousadas, Goa properties require specialized curtain applications:

Balcony doors let in light and glare. Curtains that close fully give guests darkness and privacy, and draw back cleanly to open up the view.

Heritage Pousadas

Old Goan houses have tall windows and character. A printed curtain, backed by blackout, adds colour and warmth without hiding the building's details.

Private Villas With Glass Doors

Villas often open straight onto gardens and pools. Good curtains give guests control over light and privacy in rooms with a lot of glass.

Guest Houses and Homestays

Small properties need practical curtains that give privacy and darkness without fuss, and keep rooms tidy and ready for the next arrival.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do curtains fade in strong coastal sunlight?

Over time, yes. Strong sun can fade fabric on south-facing windows. Lined or blackout curtains protect better, and rotating sets help wear stay even.

2. Can curtains work on glass doors and balconies?

Yes. Measure the full width and height of the door, and choose curtains that draw back cleanly so the doorway stays usable.

3. How do I look after curtains in monsoon dampness?

Keep rooms ventilated, avoid leaving damp fabric bunched and wash or air curtains if they smell musty. Dry them fully before rehanging.

4. Do printed curtains hide dust and marks better than plain ones?

Patterns can disguise minor marks, but they don't replace cleaning. Include curtains in your regular cleaning schedule whichever style you choose.

5. How many curtain sets should I order for a resort?

Count every window and glass door, then add spares for cleaning and replacements. Share your room plans with Meghrup and the team can help with quantities.
