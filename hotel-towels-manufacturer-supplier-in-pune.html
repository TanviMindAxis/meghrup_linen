What Are Hotel Towels?

Hotel towels are bath linen made to be used, washed and reused far more than anything in a home. One towel might go through the laundry every day of a guest's stay and then come back for the next person.

So buyers look at weight, fibre and size first. GSM, or grams per square metre, is a handy guide to density. Heavier usually means plusher and more absorbent, while lighter dries quicker. Each has its place.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Why do brand-new towels feel less absorbent at first?

Finishing on new towels can slow water absorption at the start. A wash or two usually brings absorbency up, so launder them before they go into rooms.

2. Does fabric softener make hotel towels better?

Not always. Too much softener can build up and reduce absorbency over time. Use it sparingly, or follow your laundry's guidance.

3. How long do hotel towels usually last?

It depends on the grade, how often they're washed and how they're cared for. Replace them when they thin out, fray or stop absorbing well.

4. How many extra towels should I keep for a wedding block?

Plan a good buffer, since many guests shower around the same time and laundry can fall behind. Tell Meghrup your room count and event dates and the team can help with quantities.

5. Can I order for a few rooms first and expand later?

Yes. Share your requirements and the team can advise on quantities, so you can start small and build up. Sticking to the same grade keeps rooms matching.
